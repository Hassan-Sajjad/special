Assalamualikum!

I asked you that I will not do anything after the one that you have already recieved. But honestly — you deserve something more than a text bubble lost in a hundred other notifications.

I'm not going to make this weird. You're my friend. One of the realest ones I have. And sometimes that means noticing things about you that I never say out loud — like how effortlessly kind you are, or how the room genuinely feels different when you're in it.

I made this again because I wanted you to know, even just for a moment, that someone out here is really glad you exist. Not just as a friend. Just… as you.

So — Eid Mubarak. I hope this one is soft and warm and full of everything you quietly deserve. Be always happy.

— your friend, always 🌙
